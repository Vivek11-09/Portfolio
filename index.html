Speed to lead

New enquiries wait hours for a reply

Leads come in at night, at weekends or during busy days. By the time someone replies, the prospect has already booked with whoever answered first.

Fix: instant, personalised AI response that qualifies the lead and books it straight into your calendar.

Follow-up

Follow-up depends on someone remembering

Quotes go out and nobody chases them. Warm leads go cold in the CRM because there was no next step.

Fix: automated, context-aware follow-up sequences that stop as soon as the prospect replies.

Knowledge

Your team answers the same questions all day

The answers are in SOPs, policies, past tickets and senior people's heads. New hires take months to get up to speed.

Fix: a private AI assistant trained on your documents that answers with sources and can't see anything it shouldn't.

Admin

People copy data between tools by hand

Forms to CRM, email to spreadsheet, invoice to accounting. It's slow, error-prone and expensive for skilled staff.

Fix: workflow automation across the tools you already pay for. No rip-and-replace.

Questions clients ask

You're one person. Why should I trust you with this?

Because you never have to take my word for it. You see working software on your own data every week, the build is paid 50% up front and 50% at launch, and you own all the code and accounts from day one. If it isn't working, you'll know in week one, not month three.

Do I need to switch CRM or tools?

No. I build on top of what you already use. If a tool is genuinely blocking you, I'll flag it in the audit, and the decision is yours.

What will it cost to run each month?

Usually the AI usage and hosting is small next to the build cost. The audit gives you a specific monthly running estimate before you commit to anything.

What if the AI says something wrong to a customer?

Every system has guardrails: it answers only from approved information, routes anything uncertain to a human, and logs every conversation. We test it on real examples before it goes live.

Who owns the system?

You do. The code, data and accounts are set up under your business, and you get documentation so any developer can maintain it.

You're remote. How does communication work?

Written updates in a shared channel, a live demo every week at a time that suits your time zone, and one point of contact throughout.

How do payments work?

The audit is paid up front. Builds are typically 50% to start and 50% at launch, invoiced in USD.

Tell me which workflow is costing you.

A few lines is enough. I reply within one working day, and if automation isn't the answer, I'll tell you.
